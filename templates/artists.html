How it works
  1. Turn on 🎤 Artist in Settings → Media HERE and upload your tracks.
  2. Apply to be a Verified Artist (in Settings) — or get spotted. Verified Artists wear the golden note and fans can gift their songs with tickets.
  3. Plays and likes are the road. At {{ "{:,}".format(plays_target) }} plays you show up contract-ready.
  4. Get offered the deal. Signed artists get HERE's marketing, sponsorship from the Artist Fund, and a plain-words contract — for a 3% label fee on cash-outs. Read the actual agreement →

Streaming pays artists roughly $0.003–$0.005 per play. On HERE, one fan's gift can be worth more than thousands of streams — because it's a fan handing you money, not an algorithm renting your song. And your music stays 100% yours. Always.
