🔐 Password help

People who asked for a password reset. When email sending is on, links go out by themselves (emailed). When it's off — or a send failed — the request waits here as queued: tap Show reset link, email that link to the person at the address on their account (never any other address), then tap Mark hand-sent. A reset signs the account out everywhere and works once, for 1 hour. Support mailbox: wearehereyouarehere@gmail.com.
