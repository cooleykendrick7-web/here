First, the honest part: an iPhone can't install an app file straight from Safari — Apple blocks that for every app, not just HERE. The free way in is a one-time setup with a tool called AltStore or SideStore:

  1. On a computer, go to altstore.io (or look up SideStore) and follow its setup. It puts the tool on your iPhone and asks for your Apple ID — that's how it signs HERE for your phone only.
  2. Tap “Download for iPhone (IPA)” above to get the HERE app file.
  3. Open the file in AltStore/SideStore — it installs HERE on your phone, like a real app.
One catch, so you hear it from us: apps put on this way need a quick refresh from the tool every 7 days to keep opening. The tool can refresh by itself on your Wi-Fi — its setup shows you how.
