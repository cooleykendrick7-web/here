What linking unlocks

1. With Studio Linked ON in the app, CEO can post your studio updates and screenshots of your session straight to your HERE profile — as you, through the same post pipeline as the website.

2. The app's key can do only three things: post as you, upload media for those posts, and check your link status. No messages, no settings, no wallet — and HERE stores only a fingerprint of the key, never the key itself.

3. You hold every off switch: revoke one computer or all of them here, generate a new code, unlink from the app, or change your HERE password — each one signs the app out. If either door ever closes — Creator Mode off, or the artist plaque revoked — posting pauses until it's fixed.
