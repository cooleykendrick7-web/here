How it works

1. Turn on Creator Mode in Settings (free).

2. Flip the 🍽️ Everybody Eats switch, right inside the Creator Mode card. Members wear a 🍽️ EATS badge by their name.

3. Earn at least one gifted ticket that day — on a live, a post, your Page, a ticket door, or locked content.

4. At midnight (Chicago time) the day's pot splits equally. Your cut lands in your wallet and cashes out to your bank through Stripe, same as your gifts — it shows on your earnings page as its own line.

The honesty part: the pot is a cut of money HERE already took in — never printed, never promised. A $0 day pays $0. What's guaranteed is the rule: if money moves on HERE, the creators in the pool get a cut of all of it, every day, in the open. Every payout lands on the Wall of Paid.
