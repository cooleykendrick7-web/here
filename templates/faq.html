❓ How HERE works — the FAQ

Tap a question to open the answer. Everything here is the plain truth about how this house runs.

🚀 Getting started — posts, friends, messages

Post: tap ➕ Create up top, write it, pick who sees it (Public, Friends, or Only me), hit Post. Photos, video, feelings, and songs all ride along.

Stories sit at the top of the feed and last 24 hours. Peeps are short, loud posts in the same feed.

Friends: search a name up top, open the profile, tap Add Friend. Friends see your friends-only posts and can call you. Messages: the envelope up top is your Inbox — text, photos, voice.

🎥 Platforms — going live

Every live on HERE is a Platform. Tap the red Platforms button, then Go Live — title it, pick a category, you're on air. The Lobby is the always-open Platform: walk in anytime.

Want on stage? In a Platform, tap Apply for a spot. The host sees your name and lets you in. On stage, your mute and camera are yours. Watchers can comment live and gift the host.

🎟️ Live Tickets & gifts — the money of HERE

Fans buy Live Tickets: 100 for $0.99 · 500 for $4.99 · 1,500 for $13.99, on the Tickets page (Stripe handles the card; your balance updates the second it clears).

Send gifts on lives, posts, Peeps, and tracks. Gifts leave your spend balance and land in the creator's earned balance. Every earned ticket is worth half a cent, guaranteed — the $13.99 pack gifted in full pays the creator $7.50. No follower minimum, ever.

💰 Cashing out — turning tickets into money

At 2,000 earned tickets ($10) the cash-out door opens on the Tickets page. Set up bank payouts once (about five minutes, on Stripe's own page — HERE never sees your bank numbers) and approved cash-outs land in your bank in about two business days. Bank-ready cash-outs send themselves; it's your money, there's no approval line.

🛒 The Market — buying & selling

List from the Market page: up to 4 photos, a price (0 = FREE), a category. Buyers message you in your Inbox. HERE doesn't handle payments — meet in a public place and pay the seller directly. A boost (100 tickets) pushes your listing up front.

Look for the gold Verified Vendor sticker beside a seller's name — that seller is stood-up and trusted by the owner.

🏅 Badges & stickers — the marks of HERE

★ The golden star — an important page on HERE. Apply in Settings; the owner reviews your page and posts himself. The bar: very interesting, very eye-catching, attention-grabbing.

🛡 Verified User (red sticker) — the owner has stood this person up as a real member of HERE.

🏷 Verified Vendor (gold sticker) — a seller the owner trusts; it shows on their Market listings too.

🎤 Verified Artist (gold plaque) — the HERE record label's artists. Artist mode lives in Settings.

Psst: the User and Vendor stickers have a quiet request door buried deep in Settings, near the very bottom. Most people never find it. You just did.

🏁 The $100 Invite Race (Oct 30 – Nov 15, 2026)

First place $100 cash · second 1,000 tickets · third 500 tickets. A join counts only when your invited person actually uses HERE — posts, joins a live, or uses tickets. Ghost signups don't count. Minimum 20 real joins for the money to pay out, and the owner verifies the winner before anybody gets paid. Board: joincomehere.com/invite-race.

🔒 Privacy & safety

Every post picks its audience. Settings → Privacy can make your whole profile friends-only. Block ends contact both ways; Silence just quiets somebody for you. Anything can be flagged — flags go to the owner's desk and he alone decides: remove, keep, or warn.

🔑 Account help

Forgot your password? The login page's reset door emails a one-time link to the address on your account (good for one hour — check spam). Anything else, the support inbox is wearehereyouarehere@gmail.com, run by the owner himself. Say your HERE name and the problem, plain and simple.
