🔔 Notifications

Get buzzed on your phone for new messages, incoming calls and friend requests — even when HERE is closed. Nothing runs in the background: your phone's own notification service wakes HERE for a second to show the alert, like any regular app.

🍎 iPhone: notifications only work when you open HERE from its home-screen icon (that's Apple's rule). In a normal Safari tab they don't exist. Our Android/iPhone wrapper apps can't receive them either — the browser or home-screen app is the way.
