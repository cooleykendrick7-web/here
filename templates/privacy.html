HERE

Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

HERE is a social platform at joincomehere.com where members post, go live, message, buy and sell, and where creators get paid. This page explains, in plain words, what information HERE collects, why, and the choices you have. Questions any time: wearehereyouarehere@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, your email address, and your password — which is stored scrambled (hashed), never in plain text. Nobody at HERE can read your password.
  • If you use "Continue with Google": Google sends us your name and email address so we can create or sign in to your HERE account. We never receive your Google password, and we do not pull in your Google contacts, photos, or anything else.
  • What you create: posts, photos, videos, messages, live streams, profile details, and anything else you put on HERE.
  • Purchases and payouts: payments are processed by Stripe. Your card number goes to Stripe — it never touches HERE. We keep records of what was bought (ticket packs, subscriptions, the Playbook) and of creator payouts, because that's the money ledger creators are paid from.
  • The basics that keep you signed in: cookies that remember your session, and standard security logs (like sign-in attempts) that help us stop abuse.

How we use it

  • To run the platform: your feed, your lives, your messages, your shop.
  • To pay creators correctly and keep an honest record of every ticket and payout.
  • To send the emails the service needs — password resets, a notice when your password changes, and replies from support.
  • To protect accounts and the community: stopping spam, fraud, and abuse.

What we never do

  • We never sell your personal information.
  • We never hand advertisers your personal data.
  • We never read your password — we can't; it's hashed.

Who else processes data

A few services help run HERE and process data on our behalf: Stripe (payments and creator payouts), our hosting and file-storage providers (they physically hold the site and its uploads so the platform works), and Google (only if you choose Google sign-in). They get only what they need to do their job.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete your own posts and profile details at any time.
  • You can reset your password any time from the forgot-password page.
  • You can ask us to close your account and delete your personal data — email wearehereyouarehere@gmail.com and we'll take care of it. (Money records we are legally required to keep — like payout records — may be retained as the law requires.)

Age

HERE is built for adults. Match Here, our dating feature, is strictly 18+. HERE is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update this page and note the new effective date at the top.
